Research

In-process vs. REST model serving for real-time fraud scoring

My M.Tech research. I benchmarked 11 serving configurations for a fraud-detection model, isolating the inference engine (scikit-learn, ONNX Runtime, Smile), the runtime (Python vs. JVM) and the transport (in-process vs. REST), with 5,000 calls per configuration across 3 runs.

The inference engine dominates latency: ONNX Runtime was 109–174× faster than scikit-learn, and the JVM gave no advantage over Python on the same ONNX model. Local REST added about 0.7–0.9 ms per call, and a naive Flask dev-server baseline was 5–11× slower than a tuned waitress service. Scores matched across languages to within 3.6×10⁻⁷.

Trained on the 1M-row Bank Account Fraud dataset (1.1% fraud) with a temporal split. I controlled for hybrid-core scheduling and OS power throttling, which otherwise skewed results by about 3×.
